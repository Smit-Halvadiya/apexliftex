Build a completely new, premium, modern, high-converting B2B industrial website for Apex Belts, an industrial belt and load-securing products business associated with Apex Engineering.

Reference product category:

Roll Belts
Ratchet Lashing Belts
Ratchet Locks
Hooks
Industrial load-securing and cargo restraint products

The website must NOT look like a typical old-fashioned manufacturing website. Avoid the common industrial website style of dark blue, grey, generic gradients, stock factory photos, excessive borders, boring cards, and outdated typography.

The goal is to create a website that looks like a premium international industrial technology brand — sophisticated, engineered, trustworthy, precise, modern, and visually memorable.

Create a distinctive visual identity around:

Precision × Strength × Movement × Engineering

The website should visually communicate:

Technical expertise

premium industrial technology + modern logistics + precision engineering

Do NOT copy any existing website.
